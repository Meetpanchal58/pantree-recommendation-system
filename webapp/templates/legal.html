A demonstration store

Pantree is a demonstration fashion store built to show a product-recommendation system. Products, prices, stock and other customers are simulated. You cannot buy real goods here.

Orders and payments

Checkout is simulated. No card, UPI, wallet or bank account is ever charged, and nothing is shipped. Orders, deliveries, cancellations, returns and replacements only change records in the store's database.

Your account

You sign in with your Google account. Keep your Google account secure; anyone who can use it can open your Pantree account. Please don't enter real payment details or anything you would not want stored in a demo application.

Availability

The store is provided as-is, without guarantees. It may be changed, reset or taken offline at any time, and stored data may be deleted.

{% else %}

What we collect

  • From Google, when you sign in: your name, email address and profile photo. We never see your Google password.
  • From your profile, if you add them: mobile number, city, home address, gender, age range, how you heard about us, and your email/SMS preferences.
  • From your shopping: pages and products you view, searches, cart and wishlist changes, orders, and which recommendations you see and click.

How we use it

To run your account, prefill checkout, and personalise the products recommended to you. Your activity also helps measure how well the recommendations work. We do not sell your data or show third-party ads.

Where it is stored

In the store's database. Personal details (name, email, phone, address) are kept in a separate table and are never copied into the analytics data used for recommendations. A sign-in cookie keeps you signed in for up to 7 days; signing out ends it.

Your choices

You can edit your profile at any time under Your account, and Reset recommendations there deletes your browsing history, cart, wishlist and orders. To ask for your account to be deleted, open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
